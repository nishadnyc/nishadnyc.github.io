# High-Performance Remote Desktop Streaming with Selkies

I am excited to introduce Selkies, an open-source, low-latency, high-performance remote desktop streaming platform. Imagine the experience of Moonlight, Google Stadia, or GeForce NOW, but delivered in a noVNC form factor specifically for Linux X11 and Wayland. I have designed Selkies to be embeddable within any HTML5 web interface, capable of delivering at least 60 frames per second at Full HD resolution.

## What is Selkies?

Selkies is a Linux-native streaming platform that provides GPU/CPU-accelerated remote access via HTML5. It is built for versatility, allowing for deployment across self-hosted hardware, containers, Kubernetes, or large-scale Cloud/HPC platforms. 

The project has a rich lineage, having been started by Google engineers before being open-sourced and further developed by academic researchers, LinuxServer.io, and a dedicated community of contributors. To ensure maximum compatibility and performance, I have implemented streaming over plain WebSockets by default, while offering WebRTC as an opt-in transport for those who need it.

## Key Features

I have focused on creating a professional-grade streaming experience that eliminates the lag and friction typically associated with remote desktops.

### High-Performance Rendering
*   **Zero-Copy Video Rendering:** To achieve low latency, the platform utilizes zero-copy rendering techniques.
*   **GPU Acceleration:** Automatic GPU selection ensures that the hardware is leveraged to maintain high frame rates (60 FPS at 1080p).
*   **Broad Browser Support:** The HTML5 client is fully compatible with Chromium, Firefox, and Safari.

### Rich Interactive Suite
*   **Input Fidelity:** I have implemented resilient keyboard, mouse, and gamepad input to ensure a seamless experience for both productivity and gaming.
*   **Multimedia Forwarding:** The platform supports microphone and webcam forwarding directly into the session.
*   **Enhanced Clipboard:** Users benefit from two-way clipboard support for both text and images.

### Deployment Flexibility
While Selkies is optimized for clustered or unprivileged containerized environments, it is equally capable of running on standard desktop computers. I treat any performance bottleneck that would hinder a cloud gaming experience as a bug, ensuring a high standard of quality regardless of the deployment target.

## Potential Use Cases

I have designed Selkies to cater to a wide array of technical fields where high-performance visual feedback is non-negotiable:

*   **AI & Robotics:** An essential tool for researchers studying Agentic AI, Graphical AI, Robotics, and Autonomous Driving.
*   **Scientific Research:** Ideal for drug discovery technologies and those utilizing Jupyter notebooks in remote environments.
*   **Infrastructure Management:** A powerful option for SLURM supercomputer or HPC system administrators, as well as those managing Kubernetes, Docker®, or Coder infrastructure.
*   **Cloud Gaming:** A robust solution for Linux cloud gaming enthusiasts who want full control over their hosting environment.
